Why slap-comp?

When you describe a product in a text prompt, the AI makes it up. Labels change, materials drift, and a lipstick comes out the size of a perfume bottle. Slap-comp splits the job up. You give the model the real product, at the right size, already in the scene. The model only has to light it.

  1. Upload products and click Auto Remove Backgrounds. White is removed and each image is cropped to the nearest pixel.
  2. Set the canvas size in real inches or centimeters, and add the approved background.
  3. Enter each product's height, or paste the listing text. The width is calculated from the photo, so proportions stay correct. Products stay locked to real scale, so they can't be accidentally blown up next to each other.
  4. Arrange, frame the crop, export, then refine in your image model with the generated prompt.

Canvas size

The real-world area your scene covers. Use a smaller canvas for small products like lipstick or rings, and a bigger one for bags or shoes.
